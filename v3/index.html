PCW GRANDSTAND

Prototype 3: walk, choose, watch
Draft 2Build 0.15

THE WHOLE CONTROL SCHEME

  • W A S D or the arrow keys: walk.
  • Space: do something. What you can do depends on where you are standing.
  • When a choice comes up: press its number, or click it.
  • That is all of it. Nothing needs fast fingers except one thing: kicking out of a pin before three.

WHERE YOU STAND MATTERS

  • In the middle: chops, a quick move, a power move, the ropes, a corner, or work a body part. You pick the kind of move; which one he hits depends on the moment.
  • By the ropes nearest the camera: you can throw him out to the floor.
  • In a corner: chops, ten punches from the ropes, a running charge, or whip him corner to corner.
  • Chops and the ten punches go one at a time. Press Space for another, 2 to stop. Listen to the room: stop when the WOO shrinks.
  • He's down near a corner: you can climb to the top rope.
  • He's on the floor: dive onto him, go out after him, or pose in the ring. Out there: the ring edge and the ring post.
  • Work one body part a few times, then put a submission hold on him when he's down. The room only believes a hold on a part it has seen hurt.

WHEN IT IS DONE TO YOU

  • Sell it: the job. Sell it big: his move looks huge and you stay down longer.
  • Fire back: you try to take over. He decides whether to let you, or cut you off.
  • Shrug it off: you look strong, he looks weak, and it costs trust.
  • Pinned: Space kicks out. Later is louder. For the booked finish, stay down.
  • In a hold: crawl for the ropes, fade, power out, or give up.
  • Esc backs out of a choice card.

PLAYING THE HEEL

  • Before the bell, press 2 to take The Boulder and 1 to give Stove to the CPU.
  • Thumb in the eye: he can't answer it. The card tells you whether the referee is close enough to see. Three warnings is a disqualification.
  • Bail out: when he comes at you, slide out of the ring instead.
  • Take a break outside: stall on the floor while the referee counts.
  • Feet on the ropes: cover a man who is down near the ropes. The referee may catch it at two.

WHAT THE CROWD WANTS

  • The gold line at the top says what the room wants right now. Giving it to them, at the right time, is how the rating climbs.
  • Stove is booked as the face: his job is to get cheered. The Boulder is the heel: his job is to get booed. Each man is marked on his job.
  • Shirts turn orange for Stove and gold for The Boulder. Grey means that person has picked nobody.
  • They get behind a man who takes a beating, and boo the man who gives it.
  • The same move again wears out. A choice marked "they have seen this" will land flat.
  • A comeback after a long beating is the loudest thing in the building. Cutting one off makes them hate you.
  • Before the bell, 1 and 2 swap who the CPU plays.
