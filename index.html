The cue floating on your wrestler always names the one thing to do now. The full toolbox behind it:
• Grab ties up — or covers a downed man, or (from a corner) climbs for the superplex.
• In a tie-up, push a direction to Irish-whip him (into the ropes to rebound, or at a corner), or Work to slam. Being tied up? Grab to spin out and take control.
• Hold a direction to build to a run; Strike a charging man to clothesline him.
• Work is always “do your job now” — reverse, sell, kick out, land the big one. Off the script it's a shoot.
• Taunt plays to the crowd — heat for the heel, a pop for the face.

Testing solo? Press 1 / 2 to hand STOVE / THE BOULDER to a CPU performer and play the other side.
