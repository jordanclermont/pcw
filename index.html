How To Play

STOVE HOT (face) — Move WASD · Grab G · Strike F · Taunt H · Work T
THE BOULDER (heel) — Move ↑←↓→ · Grab K · Strike J · Taunt L · Work I

The cue floating on your wrestler names the one thing to do now — its key is the bright tab. Behind it:
• Grab ties up — or covers a downed man, or (from a corner) climbs for the superplex.
• In a tie-up, push a direction to Irish-whip him (into the ropes to rebound, or at a corner), or Work to slam. Being tied up? Grab to spin out.
• Hold a direction to build to a run; Strike a charging man to clothesline him.
• Taking a bump, stay down to sell it; get up too soon and you no-sell. On a heavy move, Work to sandbag (fight the lift). Pinned? Work to kick out.
• Taunt plays to the crowd — heat for the heel, a pop for the face. Taunt right after your own move to play it up — worth far more than a taunt out of nowhere, and they tire of it if you repeat it.
• Watch the front row: leaning in and lit up means you've got them; sunk back with folded arms means change something.
