STOVE HOT (you)

  • A D move
  • F chop. On a man who is down, stomp.
  • G lock up. On a man who is down, cover him.
  • T do your part: sell a chop, reverse a lock-up, kick out, get up.
  • H taunt. Right after your own move, it is a pose.

THE BOULDER (CPU unless you take him)

  • ← → move
  • J chop or stomp, K lock up or cover
  • I do your part, L taunt
  • 1 and 2 hand Stove or The Boulder to the CPU, or take him back.

IN A LOCK-UP

  • The man who grabbed is in control: work key slams, a direction sends him to the ropes, taunt key is the finisher.
  • The other man gets a short moment first to reverse it with his work key.
  • Sent to the ropes: take the clothesline, or press chop on the way back to tackle him.

WHAT TO WATCH

  • Shirts turn orange for Stove and gold for The Boulder. Grey means that person has picked nobody.
  • Arms folded and sunk in the dark means they are bored. Forward in the light means you have them.
  • The same move again and again wears out. Stomping a man who is down gets you booed.
  • When you go down, stay down. Getting up at once makes his move look weak.
