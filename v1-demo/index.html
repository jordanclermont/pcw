How wrestling works here

Tie up (Grab when close), then push a direction to Irish-whip him — into the ropes to rebound, or into a corner.
Run the ropes yourself for momentum. Strike a charging opponent to clothesline him flat.
In a corner, Grab to go up top — the superplex needs both players to hit Work on each beat.
